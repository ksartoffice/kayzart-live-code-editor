04 / HOW IT WORKS

From the first hello
to what comes next.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you have in mind

    Share your goals, what is getting in the way, and your timing. You do not need a finished brief to get in touch.

  2. 02

    Agree on the plan

    Review the scope, price, and timeline. We agree on the details together before any work begins.

  3. 03

    Make, review, refine

    See the work as it takes shape and share your feedback. We also help you plan the copy and images you need.

  4. 04

    Launch and hand over

    We check the details, help you go live, and show you how things work. Ongoing help is available if you need it.

05 / QUESTIONS

A few things
you might ask.

Can we talk before I know exactly what I need?

Absolutely. Start with what you want to improve. We can work out what you need and what to do first, together.

What if I do not have copy or photographs yet?

We will help you plan what is needed. If you would like help creating copy or arranging photography, we will discuss the scope and cost separately.

Will I be able to make updates myself?

We plan around the changes you expect to make and explain how to manage them at handover. Ongoing support is also an option.
